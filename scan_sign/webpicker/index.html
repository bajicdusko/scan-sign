sign·scan·send is completely free — no account, no watermark on your document, no “three free signatures, then pay.” Your files are never stored; they vanish the moment you leave.

Behind it is one developer, out to kill the print-sign-scan ritual — and paying out of pocket for the server that just signed your document.
