sign·scan·send Everything stays on your device
Files live in this session only, in memory, and are dropped when it ends or goes idle. Nothing is written to disk on the server. For anything sensitive, run sign·scan·send locally — pipx install git+https://github.com/bajicdusko/scan-sign
